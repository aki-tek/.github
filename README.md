# Aki Tek
### Khmer–English OCR & Intelligent Document Research
**An AI Research & Development Organization under InnoTech**

---

## 1. About Aki Tek

**Aki Tek** is an AI research and development organization under **InnoTech**, dedicated to researching, developing, and advancing Optical Character Recognition (OCR) and Intelligent Document Processing (IDP) technologies, with a primary focus on Khmer and English languages.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between traditional document processing and intelligent automation by building AI-powered technologies that can accurately recognize, extract, understand, and validate information from complex documents.

Aki Tek focuses on solving real-world document processing challenges in Cambodia and beyond, particularly for documents containing Khmer, English, or mixed-language content.

## 2. Vision

To become a leading AI research organization in Cambodia for Khmer–English document intelligence, making document digitization, information extraction, and automated verification more accessible, accurate, and reliable.

## 3. Mission

- Advance Khmer and English OCR technologies through continuous research and development.
- Build AI models capable of understanding complex document layouts and structures.
- Improve recognition accuracy for Khmer scripts, English text, numbers, and mixed-language documents.
- Develop reusable AI models that support multiple document types and formats.
- Reduce manual data entry and document verification through intelligent automation.
- Contribute to the development of Cambodia's AI and technology ecosystem.

## 4. Research & Development Areas

Aki Tek focuses on four primary document intelligence domains.

### 4.1 National Identity Documents

Research and develop OCR models for extracting and understanding information from national identification documents.

Key research areas:
- Khmer and English name recognition.
- National ID number extraction.
- Date of birth recognition.
- Gender and nationality extraction.
- Address recognition.
- Document layout detection.
- Document authenticity and quality assessment.

### 4.2 Passport Recognition

Develop intelligent document recognition solutions for passports and travel documents.

Key research areas:
- Passport information extraction.
- Machine Readable Zone (MRZ) recognition.
- Passport number recognition.
- Name and nationality extraction.
- Date and expiration recognition.
- Passport layout and field detection.
- Image quality assessment and document validation.

### 4.3 Invoice Recognition

Research and develop OCR technologies for financial and commercial documents.

Key research areas:
- Khmer–English invoice recognition.
- Supplier and customer information extraction.
- Invoice number and date recognition.
- Line-item detection and extraction.
- Product description and quantity recognition.
- Unit price, discount, tax, and total calculation extraction.
- USD and KHR currency recognition.
- Barcode and item-code matching.
- Duplicate invoice detection.
- Structured data validation.

### 4.4 Legal Document Recognition

Develop AI solutions to digitize and extract structured information from legal and administrative documents.

Key research areas:
- Khmer and English legal text recognition.
- Document classification.
- Legal document structure detection.
- Party and entity information extraction.
- Dates, reference numbers, and registration numbers.
- Clause and paragraph segmentation.
- Key information extraction.
- Legal document search and indexing.

## 6. Research Methodology

Aki Tek follows a continuous research and improvement cycle.

1. **Data Collection** – Collect representative Khmer, English, and mixed-language document samples with appropriate authorization.
2. **Data Annotation** – Label text, document fields, layouts, and relevant information.
3. **Preprocessing** – Improve image quality through noise removal, deskewing, resizing, and image enhancement.
4. **Model Development** – Research, experiment with, and develop OCR and document understanding models.
5. **Model Training** – Train and fine-tune models using curated datasets.
6. **Evaluation** – Measure recognition and extraction performance using appropriate evaluation metrics.
7. **Validation** – Test models against real-world document samples and edge cases.
8. **Continuous Improvement** – Use verified feedback and new datasets to improve model performance.

## 8. Research Principles

Our research and development activities are guided by the following principles:

- **Accuracy First:** Prioritize reliable recognition and extraction results.
- **Khmer Language Focus:** Address the unique complexity of Khmer script and document structures.
- **Practical Innovation:** Focus on solving real-world business and operational challenges.
- **Reusable Technology:** Develop modular models and services that can be integrated into multiple applications.
- **Continuous Learning:** Improve models through research, testing, and verified feedback.
- **Data Privacy:** Protect sensitive personal, financial, and legal document information.
- **Responsible AI:** Ensure transparency, appropriate human oversight, and responsible use of AI-generated results.
